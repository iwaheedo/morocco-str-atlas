Two sources, two answers

Airbtics and AirROI both model Rabat from public Airbnb calendars, and they do not agree. Every other figure on this page is AirROI's, the lower of the two.

These are modelled estimates, not audited bookings. The gap between the two sources is a fair measure of how uncertain they are.
